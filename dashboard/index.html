Transit oversight, resolved.

One platform for municipalities and operators to manage rider reports, enforce driver accountability, and publish official service alerts across the Dominican Republic.

Request demo

For Municipalities

* Oversight across all operators & routes
* Open311-style ticketing & SLA analytics
* Policy registry & acknowledgments
* Escalations for unresolved complaints
* City-to-rider alerts (detours, works, weather)

How it Works

  1. Riders report issues (crowding, safety, infrastructure).
  2. Operators triage & fix; publish alerts.
  3. City oversight & escalation as needed.
  4. Riders get official updates—case closed.

For Operators

* Complaints inbox with dedupe & triage
* Driver roster & accountability history
* GTFS-realtime service alerts publisher
* Heatmaps, reputation scores, exports
* Multilingual comms (ES/EN/HT)
